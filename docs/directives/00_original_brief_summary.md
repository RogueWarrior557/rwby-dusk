# Original master brief: requirements summary

The user's original master prompt (2026-11-23) set up a multidisciplinary
AI studio to research, design and build an original 2D pixel-art mobile
tactics RPG. Its primary *mechanical* reference is King God Castle; it must
not copy that game's characters, lore, art or UI. This file condenses its
requirements for future sessions. Where the art directive
(`01_master_art_directive.md`) conflicts with this brief, the art directive
wins.

## Phases required by the brief
1. **Research:** pixel art and sprite craft; King God Castle in depth (combat,
   heroes, modes, economy, visuals); 11+ comparable games; a comparison
   matrix. *Done:* `docs/reports/`, `docs/research_notes/`.
2. **Three original concepts,** scored, with a recommendation. *Done:*
   `docs/design/00_concept_proposal.md`. Hushlight was approved; the
   working title is now under review ("Nothing in Faith").
3. **Deep storytelling and worldbuilding,** to the standard of a serious
   fantasy novel. *Done, in review:* `01_narrative_bible.md` (v2.1),
   `03_story_review.md`, `04_power_system.md`.
4. **Combat design:** data-driven, original signature mechanics, balance
   through simulation, no infinite loops or dominant strategies.
5. **24-hero roster.** Smaller launch roster; distinct silhouettes, roles
   and progression; no stat-swap clones.
6. **Modes and replayability:** story campaign, roguelike expedition,
   trials, endless, hub, and later possibly async PvP (only with a server
   and anti-cheat plan).
7. **Pixel-art visual production.** Expanded and overridden by the art
   directive.
8. **Mobile UI/UX:** touch targets, safe areas, orientation chosen by
   research (portrait), accessibility.
9. **Technical architecture:** engine chosen by evaluation (Godot
   recommended); modular, data-driven, testable code; a professional
   folder structure; mobile performance profiled, never claimed.
10. **Audio and game feel:** original or licensed audio. Never claim
    music exists unless the files do.
11. **Economy:** no exploitative monetisation; fair, transparent models.
12. **Roadmap milestones M1–M7,** with a vertical slice (M3): 4+ heroes, a
    boss, a story chapter, real art, animation, VFX, audio and saves.
13. **QA:** automated tests, a bug log, no "bug-free" claims.
14. **Continuous review** at each milestone.
15. **Autonomy with approval gates.** Major identity decisions need user
    approval. Keep documentation for multi-session continuity, and never
    claim background work continues after a session ends.

## Standing standards
* Never pretend research, art, code or tests were done when they weren't.
* Distinguish verified findings from assumptions, and cite sources.
* A smaller polished game beats a large unfinished one.
* "Make a game worth playing."
