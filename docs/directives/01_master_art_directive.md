1. Analyze the approved character concept.
2. Identify essential visual features.
3. Establish a readable silhouette.

6. Design the sprite at its intended native resolution.
7. Refine pixel clusters.
8. Establish consistent lighting.
9. Validate readability at actual gameplay size.
10. Create animation frames.
11. Inspect the animation in motion.
12. Correct inconsistencies.
13. Export and integrate the final asset.
