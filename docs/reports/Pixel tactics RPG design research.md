# Clarity and Fairness Should Shape This Autobattler

The evidence supports a concrete, buildable foundation for an original 2D pixel-art mobile tactical autobattler: author units on **32×32 canvases with 22–28 px chibi characters**, render a **portrait virtual canvas about 270 px wide at integer ×4 scale** with an expanding play area, and run a pipeline of locked palette, silhouette sheet, key poses, tagged export, automated lint and on-device review. A first-hand mock built in this project fitted a 7-column board of 32 px tiles into 224 of 270 virtual pixels and stayed legible at 1080×1920. King God Castle (AWESOMEPIECE, 2020) is alive and shipping roughly monthly seasons (Season 75, build 175.0.02, 7 November 2026) ([Aptoide](https://com-awesomepiece-castle.en.aptoide.com/app)), on iOS and Android only: no Steam release was found, and its only PC route is Google Play Games. Its players praise a compact combat kernel: one currency split between more bodies and more stars, merging to 7★, row and adjacency rules, run-level altar commitments, and a count-based "Absolute Defense" rule that bosses are built to break. Their complaints cluster outside combat, in stacked randomness, stamina, gacha dilution, persistent power gates, seasonal ad removal and reworks of paid-for power. Across 17 comparison games, the mechanics that add the most depth per unit of complexity all make automated combat more legible or more relational: pre-battle intent telegraphs (Into the Breach, Slay the Spire, Wildfrost), position-gated skills with displacement (Darkest Dungeon), adjacency links (Backpack Battles, Loop Hero), bounded runs (Super Auto Pets) and story that advances when a run fails (Hades). The supporting evidence points to Godot 4.7.x with GDScript and the Compatibility renderer, a portrait layout, skippable-but-recoverable story, and a monetization model that keeps randomness on the gameplay side and money on the deterministic side, which is also the direction of the EU's Digital Fairness Act proposal of 11 November 2026 ([Pinsent Masons](https://www.pinsentmasons.com/out-law/analysis/digital-fairness-act-proposal-puts-video-game-monetisation-models-in-focus)). Treat all of this as a strong hypothesis set rather than settled fact: most sources were read only as search snippets, King God Castle was never observed directly, and Godot has not yet been run in this project's container.

## 32-pixel units on a 270-pixel portrait canvas fit the brief

### Craft fundamentals reduce to cluster management under a locked palette

Professional pixel-art guidance converges on a short list of habits. Lines read cleanly when built from regular step patterns (0:1, 1:2, 1:1, 2:1) **[S]** ([OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/node/5490)). Stray one-pixel "orphan" clusters are the main source of noise and should be deleted unless deliberate, as with eyes **[S]** ([Pixel Grimoire](https://medium.com/pixel-grimoire/how-to-start-making-pixel-art-2-bcd705cb04d7)). "Banding", meaning parallel same-length segments with aligned ends, is precise enough that a TU Delft project defined it formally for automated detection **[S]** ([TU Delft Pixel Fixer](https://repository.tudelft.nl/record/uuid:b3c91b18-d9e8-48e0-91ce-8afafd2439d8)). "Pillow shading", concentric bands that follow the outline instead of a light source, fails the simple test of asking where the light comes from **[S]** ([Pixnote](https://pixnote.net/en/learn/shading)). Selective outlining ("selout") replaces uniform black outlines with colours one ramp step darker than the adjacent fill, which keeps sprites readable over varied backgrounds **[S]** ([Lospec](https://lospec.com/articles/pixel-art-outlines-part-2-using-color)). Dithering is best kept sparse, ordered and tested at display size **[S]** ([Pixel Parmesan](https://pixelparmesan.com/dithering-for-pixel-artists)). Raymond Schlitter (Slynyrd) recommends ramps that hue-shift along their length ("20 is about as high as I go", in degrees), with ramps offset around the wheel so that "every color is surrounded by colors that can work together" **[S]** ([Slynyrd Pixelblog 1](https://www.slynyrd.com/blog/2018/1/10/pixelblog-1-color-palettes)).

Curated palettes enforce much of this automatically. DawnBringer 32 ships with Aseprite **[P]** ([Aseprite palette manifest](https://github.com/aseprite/aseprite/blob/main/data/extensions/dawnbringer-palettes/package.json)), and Resurrect 64 and Endesga 32 showed roughly **322k and 182k Lospec downloads** at snapshot time **[S]** ([Resurrect 64](https://lospec.com/palette-list/resurrect-64); [Endesga tag](https://lospec.com/palette-list/tag/endesga)). The pixel-art researcher's own OKLab analysis of DB32 found the darks shifted toward purple and blue and one warm ramp rotating about 90° in hue as it lightens; that is a computed observation, not the palette author's stated intent **[I from P data]** ([db32.gpl](https://github.com/aseprite/aseprite/blob/main/data/extensions/dawnbringer-palettes/db32.gpl)). The rule with the strongest professional backing is density matching. Mixing pixel sizes ("mixels") or scaling by non-integer factors makes assets "stick out like a sore thumb" **[C]** ([Flowlab](https://community.flowlab.io/t/mini-rant-stop-using-mixels/38287)), and Octopath Traveler II's developers said they could not raise only the background resolution because "the characters would look strange" **[S]** ([Unreal Engine interview](https://www.unrealengine.com/developer-interviews/octopath-traveler-ii-builds-a-bigger-bolder-world-in-its-stunning-hd-2d-style)).

For a board holding 10–20 units, the researchers rank silhouette and cluster clarity above interior detail, one global light direction for every unit, selout instead of black outlines, and minimal dithering on units **[I]**. Character design should start from shape and colour: Owlboy's artist based characters on "shapes and colours so players can identify characters easily" **[S]** ([Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/art/using-graphics-to-express-character-and-theme-in-i-owlboy-i-)), and Celeste signals dash charge through hair colour alone **[S]** ([Reddit comment via mirror](https://axebps-redlib.hf.space/r/gamedev/comments/9a0cfr/how_is_the_hair_in_celeste_made/e4sgcdc/?context=3)). The common practitioner rule that arenas should sit in a narrower, desaturated mid-value range, so that units own the darkest darks and brightest accents, was not found in any source. It remains inference, testable by desaturating a battle screenshot and checking that every unit still separates from the floor **[I]**. This project's spike palette follows the hue-shift advice: 29 colours in seven hue-shifted OKLCH ramps plus an outline and a specular colour **[FH]** ([art spike](../research/art_spike/README.md)).

### Few size anchors are verified, so layout arithmetic decides

Hard data on sprite dimensions is thin. Square Enix kept **16×24 px** field sprites across Final Fantasy I–VI **[S]** ([Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/interview-discussing-final-fantasy-pixel-remaster-sprites-and-designs/)). The open Universal LPC character standard uses **64×64** frames, with 128 px and 192 px oversize frames for weapon swings **[P]** ([ULPC README](https://github.com/LiberatedPixelCup/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator/blob/master/README.md)). Celeste renders to **320×180** **[S]** ([Reddit comment via mirror](https://axebps-redlib.hf.space/r/gamedev/comments/9a0cfr/how_is_the_hair_in_celeste_made/e4sgcdc/?context=3)) and Hyper Light Drifter to **480×270** **[S]** ([GamingBolt](https://gamingbolt.com/our-game-is-480p-yeah-its-low-resolution-and-very-intentionally-so)), both exact divisors of 1080p. Godot's documentation says "most pixel art games use viewport sizes between 256×224 and 640×480" and recommends integer stretch scaling, available since Godot 4.2 **[P]** ([Godot docs: Multiple resolutions](https://github.com/godotengine/godot-docs/blob/master/tutorials/rendering/multiple_resolutions.rst)). No source gave the sprite size, resolution or orientation of King God Castle or any other mobile autobattler.

The recommendation therefore comes from arithmetic on phone widths. Dividing 1080 by 4 gives 270, and a design that guarantees a safe core of about 240–270 virtual pixels, then lets the canvas expand to floor(screen width ÷ scale), keeps every common device at a whole-number scale **[I]**. Table 1 shows how a 270×480 base behaves.

*Table 1. A 270×480 portrait base at integer scale with an expanding canvas [I, arithmetic from the engine note]*

| Device (portrait) | Integer scale | Visible virtual canvas |
|---|---|---|
| Budget Android, 720×1600 | 2× | 360×800 |
| 1080×1920 (16:9) | 4× | 270×480 |
| 1080×2400 (20:9) | 4× | 270×600 |
| iPhone 13/14, 1170×2532 | 4× | 292×633 |
| iPhone Pro Max, 1290×2796 | 4× | 322×699 |
| iPad 10.2", 1620×2160 | 4× | 405×540 |
| iPad Pro 12.9", 2048×2732 | 5× | 409×546 |

Two consequences follow. Leftover pixels at integer scale come to at most about four screen pixels on these devices, so black bars are negligible if the viewport expands. But a 10.2" iPad shows half again as much world width as a 1080-wide phone, so the tactical board and essential HUD must fit a fixed "safe core", with decorative bleed beyond it **[I]**. At 32 px per tile, a 6–8 column board spans 192–256 px and fits. Units of 48 px would allow only about five per row and cost 2.25× the pixels per frame, while 16 px units lose the class read that tactics depends on **[I]**. Table 2 sets out the resulting asset classes, all rendered at the same integer scale.

*Table 2. Recommended asset canvases [I]*

| Asset class | Canvas | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Units | 32×32, character 22–28 px tall, 2–2.5 heads | Headgear, weapon type, shield emblem and 3–4-tone ramps with selout stay readable |
| Elites and bosses | 48×48 or 64×64 | Scale itself signals rank |
| Projectiles | 8–16 px | Bright core with a dimmer trail |
| Area VFX | Up to 64–96 px | One full-coverage frame, then thin out |
| Status icons | 8×8 or 16×16 | Anchored above the head |
| UI icons | 16×16 or 24×24 | Integer-scaled with the rest |
| Collection portraits | 64–96 px, or a deliberately separate HD illustration style | Never pixel art upscaled to a different density |

A 24×24 unit remains the fallback if more than about 20 units must share a narrow board or the team has a single artist; the Final Fantasy 16×24 sprites show how far that size can carry **[I]**.

**The first-hand spike supports the 32-on-270 choice [FH].** The project hand-authored a concept-neutral knight on a 32×32 grid as a text file, compiled it with the project's Python and Pillow tool, and composed 14 units on a 7×10 board of 32 px tiles inside a 270×480 portrait view scaled ×4 to 1080×1920 ([art spike README](../research/art_spike/README.md); [readability mock](../research/art_spike/readability_mock_portrait_270w_x4.png); [10× knight preview](../research/art_spike/knight_v1_preview_x10.png)). Seven columns used 224 of 270 virtual pixels, which left margin for safe areas and substantial vertical room for bench and shop UI, and each character came out roughly 100 physical pixels wide: small but legible. One correction pass fixed an off-centre shield boss and a misaligned sword grip. Both defects were visible only in the rendered preview, not in the text grid, so rendered-preview review must be part of every sprite's QA. Remapping two ramps (crimson to arcane, steel one step darker) produced an instantly distinct enemy faction, which supports palette swaps for factions, rarity tiers and enemy variants. The weaknesses were equally instructive: a thin plume, a near arm that reads as a horizontal bar, and a knight that is not distinctive on its own, which makes distinctive silhouettes (unusual headgear, weapons and body shapes) a requirement for every hero. Inspecting both images for this report confirms those observations and adds one caveat: because all 14 units share one silhouette, the mock proves team separation and board fit but does not yet test class distinctness across many different heroes, which is the property the research ranks highest.

### Animation runs on few frames and variable holds

Frame budgets come from community rules of thumb rather than named studios: idle 2–6 frames, walk 4–8, attack 3–8, with attack and impact frames around 50–80 ms, walk frames 80–120 ms and idle frames 150–250 ms **[S]** ([dev.to](https://dev.to/nitin_808a59e08de0d0385ab/how-many-frames-does-a-sprite-animation-need-24k9); [sprite-ai](https://www.sprite-ai.art/blog/sprite-animation-frames); [pixelsrc book](https://scbrown.github.io/pixelsrc/book/personas/animator.html)). Weight comes from holding the wind-up and impact frames while keeping the strike brief; smear frames "work well at low resolution", and hit flashes should last 1–2 frames **[S]** ([GamineAI](https://gamineai.com/blog/pixel-art-combat-fx-hitstop-smear-frames-impact-timing-2026)). Professional pipelines key the poses first. Dead Cells' animator Thomas Vasseur adds interpolation frames "once, and only once, the animation is convincing and correctly timed with the least amount of frames possible", and Motion Twin rendered 3ds Max models to sprites so new equipment was cheap to add **[S]** ([article mirror](https://www.jikguard.com/news/art-design-deep-dive-using-a-3d-pipeline-for-2d-animation-in-dead-cells); [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/interview-with-the-developers-of-dead-cells)). Blasphemous went from a standing pose to pencil drafts to pixel-by-pixel finals **[S]** ([BackerKit update](https://blasphemous.backerkit.com/hosted_preorders/project_updates?page=7)). Sea of Stars' director notes that players read enemy attack animations to time blocks, which makes animation a gameplay telegraph **[S]** ([Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2020/03/hands_on_first_impressions_of_sea_of_stars_the_next_project_from_the_messenger_dev_sabotage)). Aseprite stores a duration per frame and exports tags with a playback direction (forward, reverse, ping-pong) in its JSON **[P]** ([Frame API](https://github.com/aseprite/api/blob/main/api/frame.md); [Tag API](https://github.com/aseprite/api/blob/main/api/tag.md)).

*Table 3. Starting frame budget for one 32×32 unit, one facing plus horizontal flip [I]*

| Animation | Frames | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| Idle | 4, or 2 ping-pong | 150–200 ms each |
| Move | 4–6 | 80–100 ms |
| Attack | 5–7 | 2 anticipation frames held 120–200 ms in total, 1 strike or smear at 50–60 ms, 1 impact hold at 100–150 ms, 1–2 recovery frames at about 80 ms |
| Cast | 6–8 | Includes a 2–3 frame loop held while VFX play |
| Hit | 2 | 1 white or red flash, 1 recoil |
| Death | 5–8 | Ends on a held corpse or dissolve frame |
| Victory | 4–6 | Loopable |

That totals roughly 30–45 frames per unit, and authoring one facing direction plus a horizontal flip halves the cost of four-direction sprites **[I]**. Durations should snap to multiples of one 60 Hz tick (16.67 ms) so timing is identical on every device **[I]**. Status effects should not require redrawn frames: run-time tints or palette swaps, small looping overlays above the head, under-foot rings, and longer or shorter frame durations for slow and haste, each paired with a unique icon so colour is never the only signal **[I]**. Variants come from palette swaps, implemented as indexed sprites plus a palette texture or a swap shader **[S]**. The LPC generator keeps one source image plus a palette JSON per item and recolours on the GPU **[P]** ([ULPC palette guide](https://github.com/LiberatedPixelCup/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator/blob/master/PALETTE_RECOLOR_GUIDE.md)), and one GameMaker tool reports that indexed sprites used about **one-sixth of the storage** of per-palette copies **[S]** ([Lorikeet](https://dragonite.itch.io/lorikeet)). Composable "paper-doll" characters attach equipment at pixel anchors that are animated per frame **[S]** ([Sigil of Kings devblog](https://byte-arcane.github.io/sigil-of-kings-website/2022/02/03/composable-sprites-part-2)), which suits the role-by-equipment idea discussed later.

VFX evidence is thinner still. The sourced rules are to keep effects on the sprite grid, use 1–2 frame high-contrast flashes, and keep smear colours simple **[S]**. Godot's `viewport` stretch mode snaps effects and rotations to the pixel grid, while `canvas_items` allows sub-pixel motion that breaks strict density **[P]** ([Godot docs](https://github.com/godotengine/godot-docs/blob/master/tutorials/rendering/multiple_resolutions.rst)). The rest is inference: give each element a 3–5 colour ramp ending near white so effects win the value contrast, fade by palette steps or ordered-dither dissolves instead of alpha, cap the screen coverage of area effects, and telegraph enemy area attacks with ground decals before they land **[I]**.

### Tools: Aseprite as the backbone, code as the assembler

Aseprite remains the de facto professional tool, with layers, frames, tags, slices with pivots, indexed mode, onion skinning, Lua scripting and a full command-line exporter **[P]** ([Aseprite README](https://github.com/aseprite/aseprite/blob/main/README.md); [CLI options](https://github.com/aseprite/aseprite/blob/main/src/app/cli/app_options.cpp)). It costs **$19.99 on Steam** **[S]** ([Steambase](https://steambase.io/games/aseprite/price)), its blog lists **v1.3.18** in June 2026 **[S]** ([Aseprite blog](https://blog.aseprite.org/2026/06/30/new-website-account/)), and its EULA permits compiling the source only for personal use or contributions, never redistribution **[P]** ([EULA](https://github.com/aseprite/aseprite/blob/main/EULA.txt)). Whether a headless CI runner needs its own licence was not verified. Pixelorama, built on Godot, reached **v1.2.4 on 3 November 2026** and has exported `.aseprite` files since v1.2.2, which makes it a credible zero-cost seat **[P]** ([Pixelorama changelog](https://github.com/Orama-Interactive/Pixelorama/blob/master/CHANGELOG.md)). LibreSprite is the GPLv2 fork of 2016-era Aseprite **[P]** ([LibreSprite README](https://github.com/LibreSprite/LibreSprite/blob/master/README.md)). The Godot Aseprite Wizard imports `.aseprite` files as SpriteFrames or AnimationPlayer tracks and can write "bake" files as a fallback for machines without Aseprite **[P]** ([Aseprite Wizard docs](https://github.com/viniciusgerevini/godot-aseprite-wizard/blob/godot_4/docs/en/1-introduction/index.md)). That matters here because **Aseprite, Godot and Unity are all absent from this project's container [FH]** ([environment constraints](../research/environment_constraints.md)).

Programmatic generation splits cleanly into two kinds. Bollinger's mask algorithm and the Sprator cellular automaton produce coherent-looking noise suited to icons, rubble and filler monsters, not a hero roster **[P]** ([pixel-sprite-generator](https://github.com/zfedoran/pixel-sprite-generator/blob/master/pixel-sprite-generator.js); [Sprator](https://github.com/yurkth/sprator/blob/master/README.md)). Professional-quality code-authored art comes from **constraint plus authorship**: hand-designed parts stored as palette-indexed grids, a fixed hue-shifted master palette, layering at per-frame anchors, automated outline and lint passes, and palette swaps for variety **[I]**. Text formats such as pixelsrc already offer semantic sprite definitions with a strict CI mode and an automatic selout transform **[P]** ([pixelsrc README](https://github.com/scbrown/pixelsrc/blob/main/README.md)), and palette tools such as PalettePainter encode Slynyrd's ramp method **[P]** ([PalettePainter](https://github.com/chickensoft-games/PalettePainter)). The project's own spike is a working instance of the authored-grid approach: sprites are written as text grids and compiled by `tools/pixelforge`, and the 32×32 result reached a clean silhouette, consistent pixel scale and deliberate value shading after one correction pass **[FH]**. Its known limits are that large sprites are slow to author as grids and that anchored parts cannot express overlapping deformation, such as a cape wrapping around a body **[I]**.

### A production pipeline with automated and human gates

The pipeline below is assembled from verified tool capabilities plus the Dead Cells and Blasphemous working orders; no single source prescribes it, so the whole table is inference **[I, built on P tool facts]**.

*Table 4. Pixel-art production pipeline for the project*

| Stage | Deliverable | Gate before the next stage |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Style bible | Virtual resolution and integer scale; 32×32 canvas with a pivot row (e.g. y=29) and maximum character height; top-left light; 1 px selout; master palette as `.gpl` and JSON with named swap slots; forbidden list (mixels, non-integer scaling, semi-transparent unit pixels, anti-aliased outer silhouettes) | Art-direction sign-off |
| 2. Concept and silhouette sheet | Every roster unit as a solid black shape at 1× on one page | Classes distinguishable; automated overlap (IoU) check between classes |
| 3. Base sprite | Flats, then light pass, then selout, then detail | Review at 1× and at target scale on a real phone |
| 4. Animation | Key poses (anticipation, impact, recovery), then per-frame timing, then in-betweens and smears, then secondary motion | Attack anticipation legible enough to telegraph |
| 5. Tagging | One source file per unit; tags `idle`, `move`, `attack`, `cast`, `hit`, `death`, `victory`; slices `pivot`, `hit_point`, `head`, `weapon_tip` | Tag and slice names validated |
| 6. Export | Command-line batch export to packed sheet plus JSON carrying durations, tags and slices; atlases per domain (units, tiles, VFX, UI) | Frame counts and durations per tag within spec |
| 7. Engine import | Animation clips with per-frame durations and pivots (Aseprite Wizard or a custom importer, with bake files for CI) | Clips play at authored timing |
| 8. Automated lint | Palette membership, alpha 0 or 255 only on units, canvas and pivot fit, orphan pixels outside a whitelist, outline continuity, banding, atlas size budget | CI passes |
| 9. Human QA | Readable at arm's length; silhouette distinct in a crowded battle screenshot; team colour readable; consistent light; no pillow shading; effects never hide unit identity | Reviewer sign-off |

A batch export built only from option names verified in Aseprite's source looks like this **[P]** ([app_options.cpp](https://github.com/aseprite/aseprite/blob/main/src/app/cli/app_options.cpp); [doc_exporter.cpp](https://github.com/aseprite/aseprite/blob/main/src/app/doc_exporter.cpp)):

```
aseprite -b unit_knight.aseprite --sheet out/unit_knight.png --data out/unit_knight.json --format json-array --sheet-pack --list-tags --list-slices --trim --shape-padding 1 --filename-format "{title}_{tag}_{tagframe}"
```

Until Aseprite is available in the container, the same gates apply to the text-grid route: author the grid, compile with `tools/pixelforge`, render a nearest-neighbour preview, and review it, as the spike showed that grid-level review alone misses defects **[FH]**. No industry naming or atlas standard was found, so lowercase snake case (`unit_<faction>_<class>_<variant>`, `vfx_<element>_<type>`, `ui_icon_<name>_16`) is a recommendation rather than a finding **[I]**.

## King God Castle's combat kernel earns praise while its economy draws the complaints

### Combat layers four small decisions on a fully automatic fight

Inside a run, the player spends **silver** both to summon heroes and to **expand the formation**. One season's deck guide grows the camp 4-4-6-7-10 across rounds 1–5, another 4-4-5-6-8 **[W]** ([en.namu King God Castle](https://en.namu.wiki/w/%ED%82%B9%EA%B0%93%EC%BA%90%EC%8A%AC); [en.namu Deck](https://en.namu.wiki/w/%ED%82%B9%EA%B0%93%EC%BA%90%EC%8A%AC/%EB%8D%B1)). Summons draw at random from a pre-built deck of **six hero types**, all of which must be filled before battle **[W]** ([NamuWiki Deck](https://namu.wiki/w/%ED%82%B9%EA%B0%93%EC%BA%90%EC%8A%AC/%EB%8D%B1)). Identical heroes merge up to **7★** in four power tiers (1–2★, 3–4★, 5–6★, 7★), which raise attack speed, spell power, attack and health, and published skill values step in fours to match **[W]** ([en.namu Character Information](https://en.namu.wiki/w/%ED%82%B9%EA%B0%93%EC%BA%90%EC%8A%AC/%EC%BA%90%EB%A6%AD%ED%84%B0%20%EC%A0%95%EB%B3%B4); [kgc.fandom Bombie](https://kgc.fandom.com/wiki/Bombie)). The resulting tension, another body or more stars on a carry, is the auto-chess core compressed for mobile, with a small fixed deck that makes outcomes feel authored instead of drawn from a shared pool **[I]**.

Space matters through simple, glanceable rules. A Giant altar bonus gives every hero in Row 1 damage reflection **[S]** ([Mejoress](https://www.mejoress.com/en/king-god-castle-best-altars-to-choose-for-your-team/)). A Great Rift rune grants Absolute Defense charges to allies within two tiles horizontally, which is why teams line up horizontally **[W]** ([NamuWiki runes](https://namu.wiki/w/%ED%82%B9%EA%B0%93%EC%BA%90%EC%8A%AC/%EB%8C%80%EA%B7%A0%EC%97%B4/%EB%A3%AC)). Grouped "Courage" heroes shield allies in a 5×5 area, Bombie's passives splash in 3×3, and one relic blesses a single tile for +35–80% damage **[W]** ([en.namu Battlefield strategy](https://en.namu.wiki/w/%ED%82%B9%EA%B0%93%EC%BA%90%EC%8A%AC/%EA%B2%A9%EC%A0%84%EC%9D%98%20%EC%A0%84%EC%9E%A5/%EC%A0%84%EB%9E%B5); [NamuWiki relics](https://namu.wiki/w/%ED%82%B9%EA%B0%93%EC%BA%90%EC%8A%AC/%EC%9C%A0%EB%AC%BC?uuid=aa8b80e2-577c-45d8-a1c1-4563d5639c55)). One hero even changes form by row, a bruiser in front and a ranged carry behind **[W]** ([NamuWiki Character Information](https://namu.wiki/w/%ED%82%B9%EA%B0%93%EC%BA%90%EC%8A%AC/%EC%BA%90%EB%A6%AD%ED%84%B0%20%EC%A0%95%EB%B3%B4)). Exact grid dimensions and maximum camp size were not found.

Combat runs in real time. Heroes gain **15 mana per basic attack**, plus more when hit, then auto-cast. A hero whose skill costs 15 therefore casts on every swing, and the developers later stopped another hero, Yeon, from recasting before the current skill finished, a sign that degenerate recast loops needed capping **[W]** ([NamuWiki Character Information](https://namu.wiki/w/%ED%82%B9%EA%B0%93%EC%BA%90%EC%8A%AC/%EC%BA%90%EB%A6%AD%ED%84%B0%20%EC%A0%95%EB%B3%B4)). Targeting runs front-to-back by default with deliberate exceptions: Lyca always strikes the farthest enemy, Bombie bombs random targets, and enemy aggro ranges are counted in tiles **[W]**. The signature defensive rule is **Absolute Defense**, a stock of charges that each fully negate one hit regardless of its size, granted at battle start by altars (two, or three at altar level 15; two to five from the Blood altar at level 10 and above) **[W]** ([en.namu Demon Defense](https://en.namu.wiki/w/%ED%82%B9%EA%B0%93%EC%BA%90%EC%8A%AC/%EC%8A%B5%EA%B2%A9/%EB%A7%88%EB%AC%BC%20%EB%B0%A9%EC%96%B4%EC%A0%84)). Counters are built in. Zero-damage crowd control ignores it, multi-hit barrages drain it, the support Mara breaks it every four basic attacks, and a relic strips it from enemies. Raid bosses variously stun the whole field without damage, "ignore all protection… and instantly kill", or hit so often that charges "almost never" matter **[W]** ([en.namu Demon Defense](https://en.namu.wiki/w/%ED%82%B9%EA%B0%93%EC%BA%90%EC%8A%AC/%EC%8A%B5%EA%B2%A9/%EB%A7%88%EB%AC%BC%20%EB%B0%A9%EC%96%B4%EC%A0%84); [en.namu Eternal Memory](https://en.namu.wiki/w/%ED%82%B9%EA%B0%93%EC%BA%90%EC%8A%AC/%EC%8A%B5%EA%B2%A9/%EC%98%81%EC%9B%90%EC%9D%98%20%EA%B8%B0%EC%96%B5); [NamuWiki Deck](https://namu.wiki/w/%ED%82%B9%EA%B0%93%EC%BA%90%EC%8A%AC/%EB%8D%B1)). No source mentions a player-cast "king" or castle skill, so combat appears fully automatic, but this was not confirmed for the current version. Battle length was never sourced; 2–5 second channels and 1.5–3 second stuns suggest fights of tens of seconds **[I]**.

Between fights, the run layer adds push-your-luck and commitment. The castle is mechanical: each stage a devil offers an item in exchange for more monsters, higher-level monsters or reduced **castle durability**, which appears to work as run health **[W; durability-as-health is I]** ([en.namu Demon Defense](https://en.namu.wiki/w/%ED%82%B9%EA%B0%93%EC%BA%90%EC%8A%AC/%EC%8A%B5%EA%B2%A9/%EB%A7%88%EB%AC%BC%20%EB%B0%A9%EC%96%B4%EC%A0%84)). Six altars (Hero, Blacksmith, Blood, Giant, Mage, Greed) level to a cap of 15 with bonuses at levels 5 and 10, which pushes players toward "max one, splash another" **[W]** ([kgc.fandom Altar](https://kgc.fandom.com/wiki/Altar)). Relics reward headcount extremes: Rapid Sword scales attack speed with identical copies on the field, reportedly with no gain past five, while a solo relic peaks at **+60% with one hero** and gives nothing at six or more **[S/W]** ([LevelsXP](https://levelsxp.com/king-god-castle/beginners-guide/); [NamuWiki relics](https://namu.wiki/w/%ED%82%B9%EA%B0%93%EC%BA%90%EC%8A%AC/%EC%9C%A0%EB%AC%BC?uuid=aa8b80e2-577c-45d8-a1c1-4563d5639c55)). The cautionary tale is the **"late team" (후발대)**, a hidden summon rule that kept designated heroes out of the first eight summons. It was meant to prevent early losses, but it "degenerated into King God Castle's most important strategy and entry barrier", became "practically mandatory" in high chapters, and was deleted on 3 December 2025 **[W/C]** ([en.namu Tips](https://en.namu.wiki/w/%ED%82%B9%EA%B0%93%EC%BA%90%EC%8A%AC/%ED%8C%81); [Arca.live guide](https://arca.live/b/kinggod/69747856?p=1)).

The main PvE progression is **Invasion**: 20 rounds per chapter, with normal rounds at 1–3, 6–8, 11–13 and 16–18, "Smelt" rounds at 4, 9, 14 and 19, elite rounds at 5, 10 and 15, and a boss at 20. Gold, Castle EXP and pass points arrive **whether the player wins or loses**, and "Corruption" Invasions cost double the supplies for about **1.6× the reward** plus exclusive tokens **[W]** ([kgc.fandom Invasion](https://kgc.fandom.com/wiki/Invasion)). One fan wiki calls this mode Raid (습격) and the other Invasion, but both describe ten chapters and a long content drought; NamuWiki says no new chapters arrived after Chapter 10 in 2022 until "Raid Season 2" on 8 February 2024 **[W]** ([NamuWiki criticism](https://namu.wiki/w/%ED%82%B9%EA%B0%93%EC%BA%90%EC%8A%AC/%EB%B9%84%ED%8C%90%20%EB%B0%8F%20%EB%85%BC%EB%9E%80)).

Why does this hold attention? The researchers' analysis, which is inference throughout, identifies seven sources of engagement **[I]**. Width against depth on one currency makes every silver a choice. Binary relic and altar commitments give runs a sharp identity ("solo carry", "army of one hero", "balanced"). Count-based mitigation creates matchups without an element chart, because hit frequency becomes a stat that few-big-hit carries lose to and multi-hit, zero-damage and armour-stripping effects win against. Each hero breaks a rule (farthest target, cast every swing, form by row, stat transfer, charge-breaking), which keeps a roster of about 70 distinct. Spatial rules can be learned at a glance. Devil's offers add an active strategic beat between passive fights. And bosses act as exams that invalidate a stock answer. The same analysis finds the weak points: too many stacked random layers, safety valves that harden into exploits, carry-centric synergy that squeezes balanced teams, hard counters without telegraphs, little or no agency during the fight, persistent level gates that override placement skill, and hidden rules that send players to wikis **[I]**.

Around these modes sits a many-currency, many-gate economy **[W]**. Castle Level is the account-wide unlock spine: Expedition at level 5, Relics at 7, Arena at 10 and Legacies at 15, with the altar cap growing toward 25, and reportedly no rewards beyond decorations after level 30 ([kgc.fandom Relic](https://kgc.fandom.com/wiki/Relic); [kgc.fandom Legacy](https://kgc.fandom.com/wiki/Legacy); [en.namu King God Castle](https://en.namu.wiki/w/%ED%82%B9%EA%B0%93%EC%BA%90%EC%8A%AC)). Permanent power stacks in at least four layers: relics in four tiers (Bronze, King, God, King God) with sub-stat rerolls at the top tier; Legacies, one per hero, levelled to 10 and then "transcended" up to 30; **Noble Phantasms**, a separate equipment gacha with time-limited "blessing" banners; and Dominion buildings ([kgc.fandom Relic](https://kgc.fandom.com/wiki/Relic); [namu.moe mirror](https://namu.moe/w/%ED%82%B9%EA%B0%93%EC%BA%90%EC%8A%AC)). Heroes come from progression, a Daily Shop at 149 gems, or summon scrolls ([kgc.fandom Heroes](https://kgc.fandom.com/wiki/Heroes)). One community source lists a Common scroll at a 2% chance of a new hero for 250 gold, a King's scroll at 5% for 35 gems and a God's scroll at 10% for 80 gems, but older guides give conflicting figures, and no official probability page was read ([namu.moe mirror](https://namu.moe/w/%ED%82%B9%EA%B0%93%EC%BA%90%EC%8A%AC); [en.namu Tips](https://en.namu.wiki/w/%ED%82%B9%EA%B0%93%EC%BA%90%EC%8A%AC/%ED%8C%81)). Blessing banners give a 50% chance that a hero's first appearance is the featured hero, every three purchase points doubles the next scroll, and one ad-funded summon is available per day **[W]**.

Four structural lessons follow, all inference **[I]**. A flat "chance of a new hero" model means every added hero dilutes the odds of a specific one, which the criticism page confirms players feel ("the odds of getting important heroes keep falling as the roster grows") ([en.namu Criticism](https://en.namu.wiki/w/%ED%82%B9%EA%B0%93%EC%BA%90%EC%8A%AC/%EB%B9%84%ED%8C%90%20%EB%B0%8F%20%EB%85%BC%EB%9E%80)). A stamina gate on a roguelike mode clashes with the replay that roguelikes depend on. Win-or-lose rewards and pass points soften failure in hard content. And week-staggered openings (Trials in week 2, Raid in week 3) give each week of a roughly four-week season its own new thing, an appointment rhythm worth borrowing in spirit, while Arena's reuse of Invasion floors and Corruption's remix of Invasion multiply content from one set of encounter data.

### Monetization leans on passes, gacha and skins; presentation is barely documented

The game is free to play with in-app purchases and ads **[S]**. The seasonal **King God Pass** costs $7.99, with Pass+ at $11.99 on the US App Store (₩9,900 and ₩15,000 in Korea) **[S]** ([App Store US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/king-god-castle/id1526791430); [App Store KR](https://apps.apple.com/kr/app/%ED%82%B9%EA%B0%93%EC%BA%90%EC%8A%AC-%EB%A8%B8%EC%A7%80-%EC%A0%84%EB%9E%B5-%EB%94%94%ED%8E%9C%EC%8A%A4-rpg/id1526791430?l=en-GB)). The pass removes ads only for its season, and a Google Play reviewer complained that ad removal was revoked when the season ended despite payment, a frustration a review digest also records **[C]** ([Gamebrain](https://gamebrain.co/game/king-god-castle)). NamuWiki calls the Noble Phantasm system "the most vicious pay-to-win element in the history of King God Castle" **[W]** ([en.namu Noble Phantasm](https://en.namu.wiki/w/%ED%82%B9%EA%B0%93%EC%BA%90%EC%8A%AC/%EB%B3%B4%EA%B5%AC)), while App Store reviewers call the game "very F2P friendly for those who choose not to spend any money" **[C]** ([App Store US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/king-god-castle/id1526791430)). The worst backlashes came from trust breaks **[W]**: a March 2023 relic-option reorganisation called "the most controversial bombshell in King God Castle history", after which the producer apologised and offered refining-based compensation ([en.namu relic controversy](https://en.namu.wiki/w/%ED%82%B9%EA%B0%93%EC%BA%90%EC%8A%AC%20%EC%9C%A0%EB%AC%BC%20%EC%98%B5%EC%85%98%20%EA%B0%9C%ED%8E%B8%20%EB%85%BC%EB%9E%80)); an April 2023 bug exploit whose punishment was seen as too lenient ([en.namu food bug case](https://en.namu.wiki/w/%ED%82%B9%EA%B0%93%EC%BA%90%EC%8A%AC%20%EC%8B%9D%EB%9F%89%20%EB%B0%B0%EC%9C%A8%20%EB%B2%84%EA%B7%B8%20%ED%8F%AD%EB%A1%9C%20%EC%82%AC%EA%B1%B4)); an August 2024 hero rework that critics said "sacrificed a hero to push special Noble Phantasms" ([en.namu Hansi](https://en.namu.wiki/w/%ED%95%9C%EC%8B%9C%28%ED%82%B9%EA%B0%93%EC%BA%90%EC%8A%AC%29)); and a resale of "limited" skins. Whether the Korean service publishes the probability disclosures that South Korean law has required since March 2024 was not verified.

Presentation is the least documented area. Only the high-level identity is confirmed: pixel art has been the marketed identity since the "Pixel Defense" and "Pixel RPG" subtitles, store copy promises a pixel-art world "where every hero, tower, and enemy comes to life with intricate detail", and a review digest praises "good mechanics and ingenious visuals" **[S/C]** ([App Store GB](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/king-god-castle-pixel-defense/id1526791430?platform=iphone&see-all=reviews); [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.awesomepiece.castle&hl=en); [Gamebrain](https://gamebrain.co/game/king-god-castle)). One escalation vocabulary runs across systems: relic tiers, chest grades and Dominion difficulties all climb through King and God, a play on the Korean slang intensifier 킹갓 in the title **[W]** ([kgc.fandom Dominion](https://kgc.fandom.com/wiki/Dominion)). Enemy families escalate by rank, as in a round of "General Lime ×3, King Lime" **[W]** ([en.namu Invasion](https://en.namu.wiki/w/%ED%82%B9%EA%B0%93%EC%BA%90%EC%8A%AC/%EA%B2%A9%EC%A0%84%EC%9D%98%20%EC%A0%84%EC%9E%A5/%EC%B9%A8%EA%B3%B5)). Sprite sizes, palette, frame counts, VFX, UI layout and screen orientation were never verified; the obvious next step is to capture footage and measure sprite heights against screen resolution.

For an original design, the line between technique and identity matters **[I]**. Small chibi units on a readable grid, rank escalation within an enemy family, elites every few rounds and a boss at the end, rarity colour-coding, damage numbers, tab-bar hubs and seasonal skins are standard across merge-defense, autobattler and tower-defense games and are safe to use. The King and God tier vocabulary and 킹갓 branding, the specific framing of a king's castle under invasion, the "Lime" enemy families, "Incarnate" bosses, "Noble Phantasm" naming, "Luminous Fate" branding and the hero designs themselves are distinctive to King God Castle and should not be copied. The transferable technique is a single escalation vocabulary used across every system, built from the new game's own words.

The pattern is consistent: the moment-to-moment rules are described as strategic and satisfying, and the friction comes from gates, odds, persistent power and trust **[I]**. For an original game, the combat kernel is the part worth learning from, and the persistent-power and monetization layers are the parts to avoid.

Read across the rows, the matrix makes three points. **Small teams with narrow, readable systems shipped some of the genre's biggest successes.** Backpack Battles came from a couple working after hours, sold 100,000 units two days after its Early Access launch and over a million before 1.0, then launched on mobile at $9.99 in February 2026 **[S]** ([PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com/games/action/steams-latest-breakout-indie-hit-is-a-fantasy-autobattler-about-how-many-magic-items-you-can-fit-in-your-backpack); [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/business/backpack-battles-sells-100-000-units-two-days-after-early-access-launch); [Shochiku](https://game.shochiku.co.jp/?p=117)). Super Auto Pets began as two developers working for about 18 months **[C]** ([Medium/CodeX](https://medium.com/codex/why-super-auto-pets-is-so-good-and-what-is-still-to-come-5c02854c675b)). Into the Breach's team cut about 60% of what it had planned, including city-building and tech trees **[S]** ([PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com/into-the-breach-originally-had-city-building-and-tech-trees-creators-reveal); [Shacknews](https://shacknews.com/article/110149/interview-into-the-breach-devs-on-influences-and-challenges)). Loop Hero sold 500,000 copies in its first week **[S]** ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/loop-hero-sells-half-a-million-promises-new-cards-and-classes)), and Slay the Spire sold over a million in its first year after Early Access **[S]** ([GDC Vault](https://gdcvault.com/play/1025667/-Slay-the-Spire-Success)).

**The recurring failures are variance without agency, power for sale, gates on replay, and volume without depth.** Despot's Game was faulted for build-defining mutations "locked behind RNG" with "no skill-based economy management" to offset luck **[C]** ([Superjump](https://www.superjumpmagazine.com/despots-game-review/)). TFT's design lead, after a 1,000-roll test confirmed the shop maths, still said "just because it is mathematically correct does NOT mean the design is perfect" **[S]** ([Dot Esports](https://dotesports.com/?p=1245514)). The Bazaar's paid-pass gating of character content, after "truly Free-to-Play" messaging, forced a Season 1 reversal **[S]** ([PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/the-bazaar/season-1-patch-notes-monetization)). Stamina gating drew criticism in Guardian Tales as it did in King God Castle **[C]** ([Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/guardian-tales-review-nintendo-switch/)), grind is the most common complaint about both Darkest Dungeon and Loop Hero **[S]** ([TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/news/i-love-loop-hero-but-it-doesnt-respect-my-time-at-all)), Red Hook walked back Darkest Dungeon II's random mid-combat relationship swings as "too intrusive" **[S]** ([PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/darkest-dungeon-2/update-relationship-system)), and Fire Emblem Engage's roughly 650 support and 1,300 bond conversations were widely called shallow **[S]** ([Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/fire-emblem-engage-has-about-650-support-conversations-1300-bond-ones/); [Twinfinite](https://twinfinite.net/reviews/fire-emblem-engage-review-irish-goodbye/)).

**Wildfrost is the closest structural precedent for a legible autobattler.** Its developers settled on "a dynamic counter system, where each action counts as a turn, and the player and enemy attack on a turn timer", and prototyped it as a physical card game **[S]** ([MCV](https://mcvuk.com/business-news/when-we-made-wildfrost/)). Units act automatically when visible countdowns reach zero, while the player's interjections advance the clock, which is effectively telegraphed autobattle with player input. Its mobile port is free until the first boss and then a one-time $6.99 unlock, and TouchArcade gave it 4.5 stars, but reviewers called the difficulty "crushing" **[S]** ([Pocket Gamer](https://www.pocketgamer.com/wildfrost/out-now-for-ios-and-android); [Pocket Gamer review](https://www.pocketgamer.com/wildfrost/review/)). The two thinly covered games add warnings rather than models. Legend of Keepers' "reverse Darkest Dungeon" defender framing maps directly onto a defend-the-base fantasy, yet reviewers found its combat too simple to carry it **[S]** ([TechRaptor](https://techraptor.net/gaming/reviews/legend-of-keepers-review)). Triangle Strategy's party votes on story branches suggest a way to tie a hero roster to narrative choice, but coverage came from one design blog and one fan guide **[S/C]** ([Flow Iconoclastic](https://flowiconoclastic.blogspot.com/2022/07/game-design-pattern-stakes-aka-triangle.html)).

## Telegraphs, relational positioning and deterministic meta complement a King God Castle-like kernel

A "King God Castle-like foundation" here means four pillars: placement on a small board, fully automated combat, hero collection, and roguelike runs. The question for each borrowed mechanic is whether it adds decisions players can read and act on, or only adds rules. Table 8 ranks the candidates. Every verdict is design inference **[I]**, but each rests on sourced behaviour in the source game, cited in the text that follows.

The strongest complement fixes King God Castle's most consequential gap: a fight that is fully automatic and partly hidden. Into the Breach's team would "sacrifice cool ideas for the sake of clarity every time", and their readability answer was to show the player exactly what each enemy will do next **[S]** ([Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/-i-into-the-breach-i-dev-on-ui-design-sacrifice-cool-ideas-for-the-sake-of-clarity-every-time-)). Slay the Spire displays each enemy's intended action each turn **[S]** ([Slay the Spire wiki](https://slaythespire.wiki.gg/wiki/Mechanics)), and Wildfrost puts a countdown on every unit **[S]** ([PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com/wildfrost-review)). Applied to a King God Castle-like game, a pre-battle preview of each enemy's first target, area tiles and divers converts "watch and hope" into a placement puzzle, and it gives bosses that "ignore all protection" a fair telegraph instead of an unexplained wipe **[I]**. Surfacing Absolute Defense-style charge counts, ranges and target lines in the UI removes the wiki dependency the researchers flagged **[I]**. The cost is a commitment to determinism: hidden critical-hit and dodge rolls undermine a telegraph pitch, which is why the researchers rank hidden random layers among the things to avoid **[I]**.

Relational positioning is the cheapest route to diverse teams. Backpack Battles makes adjacency the core decision through item shapes, "stars" and "diamonds" and recipes that form when ingredients sit side by side, previewed by a gold line before the player commits **[S]** ([Backpack Battles Wiki](https://backpackbattles.wiki.gg/wiki/Game_Mechanics)). Loop Hero's tiles transform when placed next to each other, so a 3×3 block of rocks becomes a mountain peak **[S]** ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/loop-hero-combos-cards-tile)), and Fire Emblem awards support points to units that act while adjacent **[S]** ([Fire Emblem Wiki](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/support)). King God Castle already has the vocabulary (Row 1 buffs, ±2 horizontal tiles, 3×3 and 5×5 areas), but its synergies are carry-centric. Links that reward pairs of *different* heroes would give balanced teams a reason to exist **[I]**. Darkest Dungeon adds the next layer: party order decides which skills each hero can use and which characters enemies target, and some heroes struggle outside their rank **[S]** ([SegmentNext](https://segmentnext.com/darkest-dungeon-2-party-order)). Into the Breach shows that push and pull can carry most of the depth with only three units, because its weapons redirect enemies into hazards and each other **[S]** ([Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/reimagining-failure-in-strategy-game-design-in-i-into-the-breach-i-)). Without at least two such positional drivers, the researchers expect players to converge on one optimal layout **[I]**.

Variance needs agency, visibility and cushioning. TFT's interest economy (+1 gold per 10 held, capped at +5) turns every shop into a save-or-spend decision **[S]** ([Upcomer](https://upcomer.com/teamfight-tactics-how-to-manage-your-economy-in-tft/)), and Riot added bad-luck protection for item drops **[S]** ([Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/teamfight-tactics-bad-luck-protection-items-rng/)). Super Auto Pets lets players roll for one gold and freeze an item at the cost of a slot, and its arena bounds runs at five lives and ten wins **[S]** ([Super Auto Pets Wiki](https://superautopets.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Basics); [Wikiwand](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Super_Auto_Pets)). King God Castle's own history shows the alternative: the late team, a hidden rule for controlling early summons, became mandatory knowledge until a transparent starting-hero system replaced it **[W]** ([en.namu Character Information](https://en.namu.wiki/w/%ED%82%B9%EA%B0%93%EC%BA%90%EC%8A%AC/%EC%BA%90%EB%A6%AD%ED%84%B0%20%EC%A0%95%EB%B3%B4)). Into the Breach adds the stakes model: its Power Grid, drained by building damage and persistent across islands, makes run health something other than unit hit points **[S]** ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/into-the-breach-the-kotaku-review-1823365331)). A castle or ward meter in the same role would keep the defend-the-base fantasy while letting a costly win feel different from a clean one **[I]**.

Riot's own post-mortems mark the line between depth and complexity. After its complex Shadow and Radiant items, Riot concluded that set mechanics should create excitement "without giant layers of complexity", and said it would watch and, if needed, disable augments that grant early access to deep trait breakpoints **[S]** ([Riot /dev](https://teamfighttactics.leagueoflegends.com/en-au/news/dev/dev-teamfight-tactics-gizmos-gadgets-learnings/)). Champion augments worked best when they changed *how* a unit plays and needed a specific setup, while purely economic augments were judged a miss **[S]** ([Riot /dev](https://teamfighttactics.leagueoflegends.com/en-au/news/dev/dev-tft-magic-n-mayhem-learnings/); [Riot /dev](https://teamfighttactics.leagueoflegends.com/en-gb/news/dev/dev-tft-into-the-arcane-learnings)). Slay the Spire's balance goal, every card, relic, event and monster "reasonably viable, not equally powerful", was pursued with telemetry recorded from every test game **[S]** ([Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/how-i-slay-the-spire-i-s-devs-use-data-to-balance-their-roguelike-deck-builder)). For this project that argues for small synergy breakpoints scaled to the board, run modifiers that change behaviour rather than add stats, and pick-rate, win-rate and floor-of-death telemetry from the first playtest **[I]**. Slay the Spire 2's need to patch infinite combos in its first major update **[S]** ([GamingOnLinux](https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2026/04/the-first-major-update-for-slay-the-spire-2-is-out-now/)) is a warning for any autobattler whose on-hit and on-death triggers resolve without player input.

Narrative integration is cheap if it rides on systems that already exist. Hades made "the story… advance when you died", so failure itself delivers content **[S]** ([Inlander](https://inlander.com/culture/hades-writer-greg-kasavin-on-how-he-made-video-game-deaths-drive-a-feel-good-story-22725237)). Darkest Dungeon's narrator delivers tone through short barks on crits, deaths and stress events instead of cutscenes **[I]**. Fire Emblem ties story to tactics by awarding support points to adjacent units, and its weakest moments came from volume: Engage's director confirmed about 650 support and 1,300 bond conversations, yet not every pairing has one, and critics called many shallow **[S]** ([Nintendo Ask the Developer](https://www.nintendo.com/us/whatsnew/ask-the-developer-vol-8-fire-emblem-engage-part-3/); [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/fire-emblem-engage-review-nintendo-switch-three-houses-1850059780)). The scalable version is a short reactive line at every run end, two to four authored bonds per hero earned by fighting adjacent, a small perk per bond tier, and a replayable gallery that answers Engage's no-replay complaint **[I]** ([TV Tropes](https://www.tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/ScrappyMechanic/FireEmblemEngage)). Bond progress must stay predictable and player-driven, because Hades players complain about waiting many runs for a randomly triggered line **[C]** ([Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1145350/discussions/0/4337607287061101266)).

## Supporting evidence narrows the engine, layout, story, money and feel choices

### Godot leads on paper, but only the web stack is proven in this container today

The engine research recommends **Godot 4.7.x with GDScript and the Compatibility renderer**, and the primary sources support each part of that recommendation. The latest stable release is **Godot 4.7.2, dated 18 August 2026**, with 4.8 at beta 4 on 18 November 2026 **[P]** ([4.7.2 release article](https://github.com/godotengine/godot-website/blob/master/collections/_article/maintenance-release-godot-4-7-2.md); [4.8 beta 4 article](https://github.com/godotengine/godot-website/blob/master/collections/_article/dev-snapshot-godot-4-8-beta-4.md)). The engine is MIT-licensed with no royalties, seats or activation **[P]** ([LICENSE.txt](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4.7.2-stable/LICENSE.txt)). Its pixel-art recipe is the `viewport` stretch mode, `keep` or `expand` aspect and `integer` scale mode, which "provides a crisp pixel art appearance" **[P]** ([Godot docs](https://github.com/godotengine/godot-docs/blob/master/tutorials/rendering/multiple_resolutions.rst); [ProjectSettings](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4.7.2-stable/doc/classes/ProjectSettings.xml)). Pixel art should be imported as **Lossless**, because VRAM compression (ETC2 or ASTC) "should be avoided for 2D as it exhibits noticeable artifacts, especially for lower-resolution textures" **[P]** ([importing_images.rst](https://github.com/godotengine/godot-docs/blob/master/tutorials/assets_pipeline/importing_images.rst)). The docs recommend the Compatibility renderer when "developing a 2D game", targeting older phones or exporting to the web, and it launches faster; exported games need only Android 7.0 with OpenGL ES 3.0 **[P]** ([renderers.rst](https://github.com/godotengine/godot-docs/blob/master/tutorials/rendering/renderers.rst); [system_requirements.rst](https://github.com/godotengine/godot-docs/blob/master/about/system_requirements.rst)). C# remains "experimental" on Android and iOS and cannot export to the web, which favours GDScript **[P]** ([C# docs](https://github.com/godotengine/godot-docs/blob/master/tutorials/scripting/c_sharp/index.rst)). Headless operation is first-class: `--headless` is "required" on machines without GPU access and supports CLI import and export, and GUT and gdUnit4 run tests from the command line, gdUnit4 with JUnit XML output **[P]** ([command-line docs](https://github.com/godotengine/godot-docs/blob/master/tutorials/editor/command_line_tutorial.rst); [gdUnit4](https://github.com/MikeSchulze/gdUnit4); [GUT](https://github.com/bitwes/Gut)). Since 4.4, 2D batching runs on every renderer **[P]** ([4.4 dev 3 article](https://github.com/godotengine/godot-website/blob/master/collections/_article/dev-snapshot-godot-4-4-dev-3.md)).

The shipped-game evidence is strong on PC and thinner on mobile. Mega Crit moved Slay the Spire 2 from Unity to Godot after Unity's 2023 fee controversy, and the game reportedly peaked near 575,000 concurrent players after its March 2026 Early Access launch **[S]** ([PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com/games/card-games/slay-the-spire-2-ditched-unity-for-open-source-engine-godot-after-2-years-of-development); [gamedev.net](https://gamedev.net/news/2020-slay-the-spire-2-hits-nearly-575000-concurrent-players-almost-beating-silksongs/)). Godot's showcase tags Brotato, Dome Keeper, Cassette Beasts, Halls of Torment and Buckshot Roulette mostly for PC and console, while the Android titles Rift Riff and Kamaeru are credited with crash logs that drove mobile fixes in 4.6 **[P]** ([showcase](https://github.com/godotengine/godot-website/tree/master/collections/_showcase); [4.6 features](https://github.com/godotengine/godot-website/blob/master/_data/release_4_6/features.yml)). Unity 6 offers a mature 2D stack, including a URP Pixel Perfect Camera **[P]** ([PixelPerfectCamera.cs](https://github.com/Unity-Technologies/Graphics/blob/master/Packages/com.unity.render-pipelines.universal/Runtime/2D/PixelPerfectCamera.cs)), the deepest mobile ecosystem **[I]**, a cancelled Runtime Fee (12 September 2024) and a Personal tier free under $200K, with Pro at about $2,310 per seat per year after a 5% rise in January 2026 **[S]** ([Unity blog](https://unity.com/blog/unity-is-canceling-the-runtime-fee); [Unity pricing](https://unity.com/products/pricing-updates)). Its weakness for this project is automation: GameCI documents that "Unity no longer supports manual activation of Personal licenses", so headless CI needs a Hub-generated licence file **[P]** ([GameCI](https://github.com/game-ci/documentation/blob/main/docs/09-troubleshooting/common-issues.mdx)).

The first-hand environment probes decide what can be verified today **[FH]** ([environment constraints](../research/environment_constraints.md)). The container has 4 vCPUs, 15 GiB of RAM, no GPU and no display; Python 3.13 with Pillow and NumPy, Node.js 22, OpenJDK 21, ffmpeg and a headless Chromium with Playwright are available; Godot, Aseprite and Unity are not installed. npm, PyPI, Maven and Gradle hosts are reachable, but GitHub's web pages and API, godotengine.org and Google's Android SDK host (`dl.google.com`) are blocked. A direct GitHub release-asset URL for the Godot 4.7.2 binary returned HTTP 200, correcting an earlier probe of the release page, so Godot can be downloaded; running it has not happened yet. Unity's download hosts did not resolve. iOS builds need macOS and Xcode under any engine **[P]** ([Godot iOS export](https://github.com/godotengine/godot-docs/blob/master/tutorials/export/exporting_for_ios.rst); [GameCI iOS](https://github.com/game-ci/documentation/blob/main/docs/03-github/06-deployment/ios.mdx)), and Android APKs need one environment change to reach the SDK host.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

The tension in Table 9 is the most important engineering finding in the report: **the research's preferred engine is unverified in the project's own environment, while the only fully verified stack is the web stack** **[FH, I]**. The engine note's recommended first steps resolve it cheaply: download the pinned Godot binary, run a headless import, unit tests and web export, screenshot the web build with Playwright, then allow the Android SDK host for a debug APK and spike Google Play Billing and StoreKit plugins, falling back to the web stack or Unity if a blocker appears **[I]** ([Phaser](https://github.com/phaserjs/phaser); [Defold](https://github.com/defold/defold)). Whatever the engine, the researchers recommend a deterministic, presentation-free combat core driven by spreadsheet-exported CSV or JSON, so the same code runs in the game, in headless balance simulations and in unit tests; versioned JSON saves in app-private storage written by temp-file-and-rename with a backup; atlases per domain; pooled effects; and a 60 fps combat and 30 fps menu frame policy **[I, grounded in P docs]** ([data paths](https://github.com/godotengine/godot-docs/blob/master/tutorials/io/data_paths.rst); [saving games](https://github.com/godotengine/godot-docs/blob/master/tutorials/io/saving_games.rst); [2D particles](https://github.com/godotengine/godot-docs/blob/master/tutorials/2d/particle_systems_2d.rst)).

### Portrait suits a prepare-then-watch loop, with one touch-target catch

Platform guidance gives firm numbers. Apple's default touch target is **44×44 pt**, with 28×28 pt allowed only for less important controls such as menus; system text defaults to 17 pt with an 11 pt minimum; text contrast must reach 4.5:1 below 18 pt; and layouts must respect safe areas and adapt to aspect ratios from 4:3 to 19.5:9 even in orientation-locked games **[P]** ([Apple HIG: Accessibility](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/accessibility); [Apple HIG: Game controls](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/game-controls); [Apple HIG: Designing for games](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/designing-for-games); [Apple HIG: Layout](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/layout)). Google recommends targets of at least **48 dp separated by 8 dp**, noting that a hit area may be larger than the drawn icon **[S]** ([Android Accessibility Help](https://support.google.com/accessibility/android/answer/7101858)). Steven Hoober's 2013 field study of 1,333 observations found about **49% of people holding the phone one-handed** **[S]** ([UXmatters](https://www.uxmatters.com/mt/archives/2013/02/how-do-users-really-hold-mobile-devices.php)), and Apple advises placing frequent controls near the thumb and secondary menus at the top **[P]**. No neutral census of orientation in strategy or autobattler games was found, and one developer blog's claim that "95% of mobile games today are portrait-first" has no traceable source **[S]** ([Colonist blog](https://blog.colonist.io/why-colonist-is-going-portrait-on-mobile)); King God Castle's own orientation is unverified.

The case for portrait is therefore inference, but a strong one: an autobattler's loop is "prepare the board, then watch", which fits a tall grid with the bench and shop in the thumb zone at the bottom, the enemy formation at the top, and no input needed during the fight **[I]**. Landscape pays off mainly for wide formations or a later PC version, so a portrait phone layout with a wider tablet layout, built on size classes as Apple recommends, is the practical hybrid **[I]**. The catch appears when the art spec meets the touch spec. A 32-pixel tile at ×4 is 128 device pixels: roughly 49–51 dp on a typical 1080-wide Android phone, comfortably above Google's 48 dp, but about **43 pt on a 1170-wide @3x iPhone, a hair under Apple's 44 pt default** **[I, arithmetic]**. The fix is in the interaction design, not the art: hit areas larger than the visible tile, a dragged unit lifted above the finger so it stays visible, snap-to-cell with a synergy preview before release, and a tap-to-select, tap-to-place alternative for one-handed and motor-impaired players **[I]**, all consistent with Apple's advice to show press states "even when their finger is covering the control" and to let people cancel motion **[P]** ([Apple HIG: Motion](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/motion)). Pixel fonts must render at integer multiples and still land at 11 pt or more, so body text and CJK localisation are better served by a separate native-resolution UI layer **[I, grounded in P]** ([Godot fonts docs](https://github.com/godotengine/godot-docs/blob/master/tutorials/ui/gui_using_fonts.rst)). Information must never rely on colour alone, Reduce Motion must be honoured, and haptics should be consistent and mutable **[P]** ([Apple HIG: Playing haptics](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/playing-haptics)).

### Story should be skippable but recoverable, and should advance on loss

The leading pattern in 2025–2026 is story that players can skip without losing. Honkai: Star Rail 3.4 added paragraph-level Dialogue Skip that shows "a brief summary of key events and information from the skipped segment", followed by a Story Recap for replaying completed missions **[S]** ([Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/honkai-star-rail/news/v3-4-honkai-star-rail-adds-story-skip-summary-and-character-reworks); [Pocket Tactics](https://www.pockettactics.com/honkai-star-rail/story-recap)). Arknights keeps optional depth in Archives and Operator Records unlocked by promotion, level and Trust, so lore arrives as a by-product of using a unit **[S]** ([Arknights Wiki](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Archives); [GamePress](https://ak.gamepress.gg/node/26171)). Hades' scale (21,020 voice lines and 305,433 words) is far out of reach, but its principle of short, reactive, prioritised dialogue as a reward for every run scales down **[S]** ([Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/hades-dev-script-voice-lines-huge-massive/)). King God Castle's own 2026 rebrand to "Luminous Fate" with a new story mode suggests its studio sees story as a retention lever, though the roadmap text was never read **[I]**. No public telemetry on skip rates exists; self-selected ResetEra polls found about two-thirds of roughly 945 voters never skip cutscenes **[C]** ([ResetEra](https://www.resetera.com/threads/do-you-skip-cutscenes.1325986/)), which says more about enthusiasts than mobile players. Short-form mobile narrative can be tiny and still work, as Florence's roughly 30-minute length after 22 months of development shows **[S]** ([80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/gdc-a-florence-postmortem)).

For tooling, Ink is MIT-licensed and proven in inkle's 80 Days **[S]** ([Ink](https://github.com/inkle/ink)); Yarn Spinner's core and Unity versions are MIT, while its Godot GDScript and Unreal versions use the Yarn Spinner Public License, and Yarn Spinner 3 adds storylets and saliency that suit "which bond scene is available now" systems **[S]** ([Yarn Spinner licensing](https://yarnspinner.dev/license/); [YS3 docs](https://docs.yarnspinner.dev/readme/ys3)); Dialogic 2 is Godot-native and MIT but still labelled alpha **[S]** ([Dialogic releases](https://github.com/dialogic-godot/dialogic/releases)). The recommended delivery stack, all inference, is two-to-five-minute scenes between battles with Auto, Log and paragraph Skip backed by a one-to-three-line summary authored per beat; a "previously on" card at chapter start; bond scenes earned by fighting together; a codex and scene gallery; environmental storytelling in maps and idle animations; and skip and log telemetry from the first playtest **[I]**.

### Ethical monetization now lines up with regulation

Regulation has converged on disclosure everywhere and is moving toward age-gating paid randomness. Apple's guideline 3.1.1 requires apps selling "loot boxes or other mechanisms that provide randomized virtual items for purchase" to disclose odds before purchase, and states that purchased currencies "may not expire" **[P]** ([App Store Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/)). Google Play has required odds disclosure since 2019 **[S]** ([PocketGamer.biz](https://pocketgamer.biz/news/70844/google-play-developers-must-now-disclose-loot-box-odds/)). South Korea's statutory disclosure for capsule, enhancement and synthesis probabilities took effect on **22 March 2024**, the regulator sent corrective requests to nine companies the next month, and a 2025 amendment added punitive damages of up to three times the loss with a reversed burden of proof **[S]** ([Asia Economy](https://www.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2024032208552867392); [Asia Economy](https://www.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2024040909212015942); [Legal500](https://www.legal500.com/developments/thought-leadership/punitive-damages-and-other-special-legal-provisions-for-litigation-concerning-probability-based-game-items/)). China has required disclosure since 2017, yet one study found only 5.5% of games used the most prominent format **[S]** ([Durham Research Online](https://durham-repository.worktribe.com/output/1238986/gaming-the-system-suboptimal-compliance-with-loot-box-probability-disclosure-regulations-in-china)). Japan banned "kompu gacha" set-completion rewards in 2012 **[S]** ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2012-05-18/japan-consumer-agency-says-complete-gacha-game-feature-illegal)). Belgium's de facto ban left **82.0% of its 100 highest-grossing iPhone games** still using randomized monetization **[S]** ([ITU Copenhagen](https://pure.itu.dk/en/publications/breaking-ban-belgiums-ineffective-gambling-law-regulation-of-vide/)), the Dutch Council of State overturned a loot-box penalty against EA in 2022 **[S]** ([Raad van State](https://www.raadvanstate.nl/actueel/nieuws/@130206/dwangsom-onterecht-opgelegd-loot-boxes/)), and the UK relies on 11 industry principles from July 2023 **[S]** ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/loot-boxes-in-video-games-update-on-improvements-to-industry-led-protections)). The EU's **Digital Fairness Act proposal of 11 November 2026** would show the real-money value of virtual currency across successive conversions, bar paid variable-reward mechanics for minors under Article 15 and scrutinise pay-to-progress designs, but it is a proposal that Parliament and Council must still adopt **[S]** ([Pinsent Masons](https://www.pinsentmasons.com/out-law/analysis/digital-fairness-act-proposal-puts-video-game-monetisation-models-in-focus); [mobilegamer.biz](https://mobilegamer.biz/eus-draft-digital-fairness-act-takes-aim-at-loot-boxes-virtual-currencies-and-pay-to-win/)).

The comparison games show which models players accept. Arknights earns goodwill for a soft pity that adds 2% per pull after 50 pulls without a top-rarity unit **[S/C]** ([Terra Wiki](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Headhunting); [Sportskeeda](https://sportskeeda.com/esports/is-arknights-free-to-play-friendly)). Wildfrost's free-to-the-first-boss mobile model, Backpack Battles' $9.99 premium mobile launch and Dungeon Maker's $3.99 paid app (with in-app purchases up to $39.99, some gameplay-affecting) show premium routes **[S]** ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1359652100)). Soul Knight's optional ads, gems and $1 characters coexist with a 4.59 average from about 1.7M Google Play ratings, a tracker figure, which suggests players accept that light-touch model **[S, I]** ([TouchArcade](https://toucharcade.com/2017/03/10/soul-knight-review/); [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.ChillyRoom.DungeonShooter)). King God Castle's seasonal ad removal, gacha dilution, gear gacha and reworks of invested power show the opposite. The researchers' recommendation, which is inference because economy-design sources were never gathered, is a premium price or free demo with a one-time unlock; paid story chapters; cosmetics sold directly and never randomized; no stamina gating of story; at most one premium currency, always shown alongside its real-money price; duplicates converted deterministically into shards, bond story or cosmetic tiers; no rewards for completing sets obtainable only through paid draws; and randomness confined to the gameplay side, in shop rolls and battle drops, where paid-loot-box rules do not reach **[I]**. If a gacha is commercially unavoidable, it needs full odds and pity disclosure, a deterministic "spark", no purchases by minors and real-money pricing **[I]**.

### Game feel should clarify outcomes and stay switchable

The canonical juice references are Jonasson and Purho's "Juice It or Lose It" (GDC Europe 2012), Nijman's "The Art of Screenshake" (2013, about 30 small tweaks) and Eiserloh's trauma-based camera shake (GDC 2016) **[S]** ([GDC Vault](https://gdcvault.com/play/1016487/Juice-It-or-Lose); [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/vlambeer-co-founder-shares-advice-on-building-better-action-games); [GDC Vault](https://gdcvault.com/play/1023146/Math-for-Game-Programmers-Juicing)). Implementations of the trauma model accumulate a 0–1 value, decay it each frame, derive displacement from its square or cube, and sample Perlin noise **[S]** ([Bevy example](https://bevy.org/examples/camera/2d-screen-shake/); [KidsCanCode](https://kidscancode.org/godot_recipes/3.x/2d/screen_shake/)). Sakurai's hit-stop rule freezes both attacker and target and caps the freeze "regardless of how much damage an attack inflicts" **[S]** ([Source Gaming](https://sourcegaming.info/2015/11/11/thoughts-on-hitstop-sakurais-famitsu-column-vol-490-1/)); Smash Bros. caps hitlag at 20 frames in Melee and 30 from Brawl onward **[S]** ([SSBWiki](https://www.ssbwiki.com/Hitlag)). A later talk warned that piling on polish can cost immersion **[S]** ([Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/video-indies-resist-the-urge-to-juice-it-or-lose-it-)). Accessibility guidance requires restraint: Apple warns against gratuitous motion and fast blinking, Xbox guideline 117 asks for shake, bobbing and blur to be avoidable (citing Halo Infinite's 0–100% sliders), and guideline 118 requires photosensitivity testing **[P/S]** ([Apple HIG: Motion](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/motion); [XAG 117](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/gaming/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/117); [XAG 118](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-ie/gaming/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/118)).

Because autobattle combat is mostly watched, feel should clarify outcomes more than reward inputs **[I]**. That means per-unit hit-stop of about 2–6 frames at 60 fps (33–100 ms) on crits, kills and ultimates only, with a hard cap; trauma shake reserved for ultimates and boss phases, under a global slider that honours Reduce Motion; 1–2 frame palette flashes rather than full-screen flashes; damage numbers that merge per unit and carry icons as well as colour; a "battle readability" preset with fewer particles and no shake; and particle density tied to the battery setting **[I]**. Audio-tool licensing is clear where it matters: ChipTone output is CC0, jfxr asks for no attribution, and Kenney assets are CC0, while Freesound and OpenGameArt licenses vary per file and Freesound's non-commercial files cannot ship in a commercial game **[S]** ([ChipTone](https://sfbgames.itch.io/chiptone); [jfxr](https://github.com/ttencate/jfxr); [Kenney](https://kenney.nl/support); [Cinevva](https://app.cinevva.com/guides/game-asset-licenses)). Audio practice itself (layered impacts, pitch variation, music ducking, adaptive layering and leitmotifs) was never sourced and remains inference.

## Conclusion

The research changes the framing of the design problem. The binding constraint for a pixel-art mobile autobattler is legibility, not fidelity: the 32-pixel unit on a 270-pixel canvas, the pre-battle telegraph, the visible charge counter, the adjacency preview and the switchable juice all serve the same goal of letting a player understand an automated fight well enough to own its outcome. King God Castle's own trajectory supports this. Its players forgive automation but not opacity or monetized randomness, and its most important recent fixes (deleting the hidden late-team rule, promising to review level gates) moved toward transparency. An original game can differentiate precisely where King God Castle draws complaints, by putting randomness on the gameplay side and money on the deterministic side, and that same split is where regulators from Seoul to Brussels are steering the industry. One concrete tension surfaced only by combining the notes: the art spec that reads well on screen produces unit cells just under Apple's 44 pt touch target on common iPhones, so the interaction layer, not the sprite size, must absorb the difference.

The evidence base should now be treated as a hypothesis set to be tested first-hand rather than researched further from snippets. Three tests would retire most of the remaining uncertainty: capture and measure King God Castle footage (sprite size, orientation, grid, battle length, any player-cast skill); run Godot 4.7.2 headless in the container through import, tests, web export and Playwright screenshots, then spike Android billing; and extend the art spike from one knight to a silhouette sheet of eight to ten distinct heroes on the 270-pixel board, since class distinctness, not board fit, is now the untested property that matters most.
